Trust & safety — the moat

Engineered to be trusted in your home.

A robot that lives with your family has one job before all others: be safe. Trust and safety are our first engineering requirement — especially for homes with kids, elderly parents, and wheelchair users.

Stable by design

A statically stable base. It never balances on two legs around your family. It can't tip into a child, an elderly parent, or a wheelchair.

Hardware emergency stop

A physical e-stop cuts motor power directly. Not software. Not an app. A switch.

Gentle by default

Current-limited joints, capped speeds, and workspace limits. It is physically restrained from moving in ways that could hurt.

Independent safety supervisor

A separate safety computer watches the main system and can halt motion on its own.

Private by architecture

Powered by Atraly, our personal AI division. Your home is your business: data stays yours.

Safety isn't a sticker on the box. It's the architecture.
